# Standard deduction amounts, tax year 2025 (official IRS source)
Source: IRS VITA/TCE training content, "Standard deduction amounts (2025)" - https://apps.irs.gov/app/vita/content/00/00_13_005.jsp
Retrieved: 2026-11-14. Tax year 2025. Federal only.

| Filing status | 2025 standard deduction |
|---|---|
| Married filing jointly or qualifying surviving spouse | $31,500 |
| Head of household | $23,625 |
| Single or married filing separately | $15,750 |

## Additional amount for age 65 or older, or blind
- Single or head of household: $2,000
- Married taxpayers or qualifying surviving spouse: $1,600
(The source page does not list a separate amount for married filing separately; it says only "married taxpayers.")

## Taxpayer who can be claimed as a dependent
The standard deduction cannot exceed the greater of (1) $1,350, or (2) the sum of $450 and the individual's earned income.

## Note on dependents
The standard deduction depends on filing status, not on the number of dependents. A married-filing-jointly return gets $31,500 whether it has 0, 2 or 5 dependents.
