# IRA income limits 2025
Source: "IRA income limits 2025.docx" (three table images, transcribed to text). Tax year 2025.

## 2025 IRA deduction limits: you are NOT covered by a retirement plan at work
| Filing status | Modified adjusted gross income (MAGI) | Deduction limit |
|---|---|---|
| Single, head of household, or qualifying widow(er) | Any amount | Full deduction up to the amount of your contribution limit |
| Married filing jointly with a spouse who is not covered by a plan at work | Any amount | Full deduction up to the amount of your contribution limit |
| Married filing jointly with a spouse who is covered by a plan at work | $236,000 or less | Full deduction up to the amount of your contribution limit |
| Married filing jointly with a spouse who is covered by a plan at work | More than $236,000 but less than $246,000 | Partial deduction |
| Married filing jointly with a spouse who is covered by a plan at work | $246,000 or more | No deduction |
| Married filing separately with a spouse who is covered by a plan at work | Less than $10,000 | Partial deduction |
| Married filing separately with a spouse who is covered by a plan at work | $10,000 or more | No deduction |

## 2025 IRA deduction limit: you ARE covered by a retirement plan at work
| Filing status | Modified adjusted gross income (MAGI) | Deduction limit |
|---|---|---|
| Single individuals | $79,000 or less | Full deduction up to the amount of your contribution limit |
| Single individuals | More than $79,000 but less than $89,000 | Partial deduction |
| Single individuals | $89,000 or more | No deduction |
| Married (filing joint returns) | $126,000 or less | Full deduction up to the amount of your contribution limit |
| Married (filing joint returns) | More than $126,000 but less than $146,000 | Partial deduction |
| Married (filing joint returns) | $146,000 or more | No deduction |
| Married (filing separately) | Less than $10,000 | Partial deduction |
| Married (filing separately) | $10,000 or more | No deduction |

## Roth IRA income requirements for 2025
| Filing status | Modified adjusted gross income (MAGI) | Contribution limit |
|---|---|---|
| Single individuals | Less than $150,000 | $7,000 |
| Single individuals | $150,000 or more but less than $165,000 | Partial contribution |
| Single individuals | $165,000 or more | Not eligible |
| Married (filing joint returns) | Less than $236,000 | $7,000 |
| Married (filing joint returns) | $236,000 or more but less than $246,000 | Partial contribution |
| Married (filing joint returns) | $246,000 or more | Not eligible |
| Married (filing separately) | Less than $10,000 | Partial contribution |
| Married (filing separately) | $10,000 or more | Not eligible |
