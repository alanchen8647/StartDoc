# Steps and Conversations: Walk-in Clients

### 1 st client walk-in #1
Go to supervisor’s desk

### 1 st client walk-in #2
supervisor checks documents and chat briefly, tell clients you will see them, check return, & answer his questions shortly after your assistant entering all basic information. Send them to your assistant for next step.

### 1 st client walk-in #3
assistant enters all you know & learned, ask all necessary questions to finish clients’ tax returns. Also, show them our additional tax service menu. Only answer the questions you are 100% confident. Otherwise tell clients your supervisor will answer their questions during reviewing time. After finishing data processing, send client back to supervisor.

### 1 st client walk-in #4
supervisor: enter the rest, review return, answer client’s concern, calculate and collected fee. Give everything back to assistant. Assistant continue follow up such as missing signature, missing documents etc.

### 2 nd client walk-in to the same supervisor #1
Go to supervisor’s desk

### 2 nd client walk-in to the same supervisor #2
supervisor checks documents and chat briefly, tell clients you will see them, check return, & answer his questions shortly after your assistant entering all basic information, and send client to other supervisor’s assistant to process this clients’ data.

### 2 nd client walk-in to the same supervisor #3
assistant enters all you know & learned, ask all necessary questions to finish clients’ tax returns. Also, show them our additional tax service menu. Only answer the questions you are 100% confident. Otherwise tell clients your supervisor will answer their questions during reviewing time. After finishing data processing, send client back to the originated supervisor.

### 2 nd client walk-in to the same supervisor #4
Originated supervisor: enter the rest, review return, answer client’s concern, calculate and collected fee. Give everything back to the entering assistant. Entering assistant continue follow up such as missing signature, missing documents etc. E-file by assistance’s supervisor. NOT originated supervisor.

### 3 rd client walk-in to the same supervisor #1
Go to supervisor’s desk

### 3 rd client walk-in to the same supervisor #2
supervisor checks documents and chat briefly, tell clients you will see them, check return, & answer his questions shortly after your assistant entering all basic information, and send client to YOUR OWN assistant to process this clients’ data, and you take your 1 st client back to your desk. And your own assistant work on your 3rd client case as 1st client. You finish up the rest for 1st client and for your own assistant.
