# Conversation Script: Personal Tax Return Call

## Personal income tax only returns

WC: Hello, W C Tax & Consulting. How can I help you?

Client: How much will cost me to file an income tax return and what documents to bring?

WC: Typically, Our fee is around $100 - $200. It depends on your household composition and how many tax forms you have.

Client: I have xxx,xxx,xxx,xxxx,……………………….

WC: Okay, your range should be 100,200 / 200,300 / 300,400 / 400,500. Can you upload or send your paper to us to get an exact quote? I can send you a $50 rolled over coupon after you send your documents. (our intention is let them send over the documents).

If he/she continues to ask questions. Tell him we are busy at this moment please upload/send over documents, and we will get back to them.

If he/she stops asking questions. Provide him a method(portal, Wechat, Whatsapp, text message, email) to send documents.
