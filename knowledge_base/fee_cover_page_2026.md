# W.C. Tax and Consulting Service Inc. - 2026 Cover Page (service fee sheet)
Source: "2026 cover page 2.pdf" (2 pages, a checkbox form, transcribed to text). Applies to the 2026 filing season.
The printed heading "Minimum charge for certain groups" refers to the Minimum Fee Letter (income over $200,000 = $300 minimum, over $400,000 = $400, over $500,000 = $500). Those minimums are a separate rule and are not part of this service fee table: the fees below are the regular service fees, and the income minimums are not applied when adding up this table.

## Page 1 - header notes
- All tax information is provided by the taxpayer. W.C. Tax and Consulting does not provide tax advice or fictitious strategies.
- Additional fees will be charged for tax examination or representation services.
- The top of the form has a refund / amount-you-owe box (federal, state, other state, total), filled in per client.

## Fee table (as printed, one line per cell of the form)
### Filing status (base fee) - confirmed by the office
- Single: starts at $100. No dependent fee and no EITC fee is charged.
- Married Filing Jointly without Dependent: starts at $110. This is for MFJ clients with no dependents. No dependent fee and no EITC fee is charged ("Including EITC" on the form).
- Married Filing Jointly, Married Filing Separately, or Head of Household (the normal case): starts at $110. The dependent fee and the EITC fee below are added on top when they apply.
### Dependents and credits
- Dependent: $5 each (charged only for MFJ, MFS or Head of Household clients, not for Single or MFJ without dependent)
- Earned Income Tax Credit: $20 (same rule: charged only for MFJ, MFS or Head of Household clients)
- Child Tax Credit or ODC: $10
- Dep Care Credit (Fed/NY): $25
- Education Credit or 1095A: $25 each
### Schedules and forms
- Schedule A: $40
- Additional State: $25 each
- W2 (more than 2): $5 each
- Schedule C / NEC (each job): no expenses $30 | with expenses under $50K $70 | expenses over $50K $120
- SMLLC: $100
- K1 (each): $25 by default, or $50 for a more complex K1. The preparer decides how complex it is when they actually see the K1, so quote $25 and tell the client the K1 price is to be determined when the K1 is reviewed.
- Schedule D: $25 for fewer than 3 trades | $40 each PDF | $70 each Excel
- Schedule E: $65 each unit
- 1040X (amended return): $90
- IRA, W2G, 1116, HSA, 8880: [1-2] $10 | [3-4] $20 | [5-6] $30
- Energy Related Credits: $50 each
- 1098s & 1099s: $5 each
- Simple Dependent Return: $60
- Foreign Income/Acc: $85 each
- Senior Tax Return: $50 (with rent paid: $60)
- Tips / Overtime / Car loan: $20
- Trump Acc: $40
### Discount and total
- Discount: 2025 Client Cash/Zelle: -$10
- Other: +/- $ (written in by the preparer)

## Page 2 - services and other fees
### Tax services
Personal tax return, business tax return, ITIN application, gift tax, trust tax, overseas asset reporting, audit representation, company formation, business licensing, tax saving strategy.

### Tax planning
For detailed tax planning, the service fee is $400 per hour. The firm studies the client's tax situation in detail and provides the most favorable and legal tax saving plan, and advises how to legally obtain more tax refunds and benefits from various government agencies.

### Insurance services
Homeowners Insurance, Commercial Insurance, Workers' Compensation Insurance, Life Insurance.

### Reprint fee
If you lose your copy, the reprint fee is $20. The firm can email an encrypted file at no additional charge.

## Reading notes (all confirmed by the office)
1. Filing status: Single ($100) and MFJ without dependent ($110) are all-inclusive starting prices. MFJ, MFS and Head of Household start at $110, and the dependent ($5 each) and EITC ($20) fees are added when they apply.
2. Schedule C/NEC: the middle price ($70) is a normal Schedule C/NEC that has expenses, but under $50K.
3. K1 (each): $25 by default. The worker decides on $50 for complex K1s when they see it, so the price is "to be determined" until then.
4. The heading "Minimum charge for certain groups" refers to the Minimum Fee Letter (over $200K / $400K / $500K), which is a separate rule and is not considered when totaling the service fee table.
5. The office manual says very complex new-client cases are priced by time and difficulty, not by this cover page.
