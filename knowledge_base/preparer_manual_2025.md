# Tax Season Preparer Manual

## The beginning

Phase 1 Watch & observe your supervisor how to do cases from the beginning to the end (5 cases).

Phase 2 Supervisor reviews client case and papers, assign it to you, once you finish, supervisor reviews and calculates fee, finally you collect documents, signature, and payment (15 cases)

Phase 3 work on your own cases. enter all tax forms as you know how to. Leave all questionable items to your supervisor such as tax form, software, clients’ questions if necessary. Collect documents, signatures, and payment.

Assist your supervisor to follow up his/her client documents, signature, payment, and anything easy to handle.

You can ask your supervisor and me when you need help (for cases questions. please after entered all data).

Enter all tax forms as you know how to. Remember to enter all tax forms and personal information first. Leave all questionable items to your supervisor such as tax forms, software/hardware, clients’ questions at the end. If you don’t know the answer, don’t make it up. Say I will ask my supervisor when he/she is available.

## Make sure collect documents for the following credits/status

- Earn income tax credits: proof of address & relationship (such doctor letter/school letter/medical record)
- Child tax credit with NO EIC: Insurance form such as 1095A with kids’ names (most of them have this paper) or anything from EIC list

## For EIC with self-employment (be very careful)

- File as single; has either proof of income or expense = Claim EIC
- File as MJF or HOH; must provide both proof of income & expense to claim EIC.

## For filing head of household status

- If divorce? We need a court letter.
- Married but live apart for over 6 months? write this note in Drake: Client confirms that he/she did not live with spouse over 6 months, paid all household expenses, and lived with his/her dependents over 6 months last year.
- Never married? write this note in Drake: Client confirms that he/she wasn’t married last year, paid all household expenses, and lived with his/her dependents over 6 months last year.

## Education Credits

- Proof of education expenses – know the rules for AOTC and LLC

## Additional items to pay attention

- Know IT-214 Real Property Tax Credit for Homeowners and Renters for both elderly and low-income household. IT-229 is gone.
- Don’t double mortgage interest paid & property tax paid deductions on Sch A and Sch E.
- Make sure you understand 4 types of investment input methods clearly.

## Recording

- Scan all tax forms & proofs into drake client document folder (cut and paste only. Don’t leave them in scan folder)
- record client list paper such as payment or missing document/payment.
- Continue to check your client list paper during the tax reason. Make sure put accepted “A” status next to client name.

## Printing

- Always two side print.
- Only print tax forms, signature pages, and engagement letter. Don’t print other papers such as worksheets.
- Collect signature pages.

## Signature

- 1, Sign on papers 2, send over through email or WeChat (*these PDF files must be password protected) 3, send secured E sign through drake software with token

- Square system for credit card & debt card payment (or drake portal)
- We also offer Venmo & Paypal. Use QR codes.
- Zelle payment. Use office cellphone number 929 326 3045
